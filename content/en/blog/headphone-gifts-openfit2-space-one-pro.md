## Start with how the recipient listens

Ask one practical question first: would the recipient choose an ear-hook design that leaves the ear open, or earcups and a headband for seated listening and travel? This affects everyday use more than matching every employee to one model.

For a mixed team, collect the preferred format and colour before freezing the order. A recipient who already has a favourite headset may prefer another direction from our [technology gift guide](/guides/premium-technology-gifts/). Keep the gift choice separate from assumptions about age, job title or nationality.

## OpenFit 2: an open-ear direction

[Shokz OpenFit 2](/#gift-shokz-openfit) uses air-conduction audio in an open-ear format. Shokz lists physical multifunction buttons and multipoint pairing for two devices. Its product listing includes a charging case and USB-C cable. Specify standard OpenFit 2 when reviewing the proposed model.

Use the sample to check the ear-hook fit, button access and the way the earbuds sit with the recipient’s glasses. If phone-to-laptop switching matters, demonstrate that sequence with the intended devices. Keep the charging case with the earbuds in the gift layout and allow easy access when taking them out.

## Space One Pro: over-ear listening and folding

[Soundcore Space One Pro](/#gift-soundcore-space) is an over-ear model with active noise cancellation and a folding FlexiCurve structure. It is a direction to consider for a recipient who wants this format during business travel or seated listening.

Review headband adjustment, earcup fit and the folding sequence on the sample. For someone seeking quieter listening, compare the available listening modes in the intended setting. Confirm the actual carrying accessories and packed dimensions; the gift box should accommodate the approved configuration without pressing on the hinges or controls.

## Check the complete phone and work-call setup

Record the phone and, if relevant, the work laptop used by the recipient. Check current model compatibility and the required app access before treating a Bluetooth feature as a complete workplace solution. For managed laptops, ask the organisation which pairing and app-installation options are available.

The useful acceptance result is a repeatable task: connect the proposed device, place a call in the normal work app, select the intended microphone and speakers, then check reconnecting after the headset has been put away. Test any requested two-device switching on that same setup. Keep any unresolved requirement in the proposal.

- Model and package: exact name, regional version, colour, contents and support route.
- Fit and controls: comfortable wearing position, glasses if used, accessible buttons and adjustment.
- Connectivity: intended phone/laptop, pairing, call routing and any required switching.
- Charging and carrying: supplied cables, compatible power source, case or pouch and clear instructions.

## Give the occasion a clear message

For Eid employee appreciation, offer the two wearing formats as a preference choice and use a shared Eid greeting. For a service anniversary, personalise the card with the person’s approved name and milestone. For an Eid Al Etihad recognition programme, tie the message to the occasion and the contribution being recognised.

For a client who travels regularly, confirm the preferred headset format and carrying arrangement. Use our [UAE gifting calendar](/guides/uae-corporate-gifting-calendar/) for the campaign context, then work backwards from the actual office distribution, event or departure date. Agree the choice deadline, artwork proof, assembled sample, packing and handover as separate steps.

## One company identity, two properly fitted presentations

MUSE can develop a common sleeve, colour direction and Arabic–English card while fitting each model to its own insert. Keep the retail product, charging or carrying components and instructions together. Device decoration is confirmed for the exact item; start the visual identity on the outer presentation.

Review an assembled gift for each selected model. Check removal from the insert, reopening the charging case or folding the headset, and matching the name card to the recipient’s choice.
