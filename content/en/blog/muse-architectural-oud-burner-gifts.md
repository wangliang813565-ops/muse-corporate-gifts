## Match the occasion to a recipient who will value the piece

For a Ramadan business visit, consider an oud burner when the host enjoys this kind of hospitality object. For Eid client appreciation, a personal dedication can make the same architectural direction specific to the relationship. For Eid Al Etihad or a heritage-themed recognition event, start with the architectural form and develop the greeting around the occasion.

Ask whether the gift is intended for the recipient’s home or another agreed setting. A mixed employee list may need a choice alongside [premium technology gifts](/guides/premium-technology-gifts/). Use our [Ramadan and Eid guide](/guides/ramadan-eid-corporate-gifts/) to separate host, individual and family briefs.

## Compare the three forms before choosing a finish

View all three in our [architectural oud-burner collection](/#gift-oud-majlis). The design views show different silhouettes; they do not establish actual dimensions or operating specifications. Compare their outline with the style the recipient likes, then confirm size on a physical sample.

- **Wind Crown:** a rounded tower, repeated arched panels and a crown-like upper edge. Start here for a tall, unified silhouette.
- **Parapet Ribbon:** a curved-wall body with an upper parapet band and a softer, less symmetrical outline. Start here when the brief favours flowing shapes.
- **Courtyard Keep:** a rectangular body framed by four corner towers, with arched panels and a patterned band. Start here for a more structured architectural composition.
- These are three forms within our original oud-burner direction. Select the form by name so the proposal, artwork and sample all refer to the same design.

## Build a colour direction around the company identity

The collection’s champagne-and-teal treatment is one starting point. We can also explore silver and navy, lilac, or rose and burgundy directions. Bring the company’s colour references and decide which element carries the strongest accent: the leather panels, presentation lining or greeting card.

Use the metal finish and leather colour as separate decisions. A colour name alone does not specify the material, surface texture or exact shade. Review finish references alongside the proposed box and approve the selected combination on a sample.

## Decide where the logo and dedication belong

For a personal VIP gift, consider a restrained outer identity with the recipient’s dedication inside the case. For a coordinated client campaign, use a consistent presentation style while changing the names and greetings. Confirm any marking on the object separately, including placement, scale and method.

MUSE can develop the original design direction, finish selection, presentation case and bilingual dedication together. Our [customisation guide](/guides/corporate-gift-customisation/) explains this route alongside branded-product presentation. A new body shape needs its own development and sample review; changing a card does not require the same decisions.

## Approve the working object and the complete packed gift

For this design, the useful sample review has two parts: how the burner will be used, and how the recipient will receive it. Agree the intended operating arrangement, heat-handling components and instructions before approving production. The design images alone cannot answer those questions.

- **Object:** confirm dimensions, materials, finish, stability, intended use, heat-handling components and care instructions on the physical sample.
- **Contents:** state exactly what is included. Specify separately whether oud, any consumables or accessories form part of the gift.
- **Presentation:** check the fitted insert, protection around the upper details and corners, and how the object is removed from the case.
- **Personalisation:** review Arabic and English names, dedication, logo placement and the matching between each recipient and their box.
- **Approval:** retain the accepted specification and sample reference so later changes can be reviewed against them.

## Plan backwards from the actual handover

Use the [UAE gifting calendar](/guides/uae-corporate-gifting-calendar/) to choose the campaign occasion, then give us the actual meeting or distribution date. Form selection, finish references, sample approval, packing and delivery each need a place in the project plan; the schedule is agreed for the selected design and quantity.

Send your recipient groups, occasion, quantities, chosen form, colour references, dedication language and required handover date through our [project brief](/#gift-brief). Add the intended setting and requested contents so we can develop a complete proposal.
