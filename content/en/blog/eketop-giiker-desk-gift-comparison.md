## Start with the moment after the box is opened

A founder may place a gift beside a notebook; an employee may take it home; an event guest may try it at a demonstration table. These are different uses. Ask whether the recipient wants to watch movement, solve something alone or invite another person to play.

Our [desk and play collection](/#gift-selection) includes all three directions. A coordinated campaign can offer a choice while keeping the same greeting and packaging style. Set quantities for each option before approving the complete gift.

- Adult desk and design interest: begin with EKETOP.
- An individual who enjoys a repeatable puzzle challenge: compare Super Slide.
- A household or shared activity with space for a board: compare Smart Four.

## EKETOP: make the object and its presentation the focus

The [EKETOP kinetic metal spinning top](/#gift-eketop) is the display-led choice. Its attraction is the physical object, movement and the way it sits on a desk. It can suit a design-minded client or an innovation-event gift when that experience fits the recipient.

Review the actual model on its intended base or surface. Check how it starts, how it is handled and how it returns to its presentation case. Choose the size and finish from a sample rather than a video alone; agree any operating-time claim for the exact model before putting it into campaign copy.

- Compare the surface finish, edges, weight and desk footprint in person.
- Confirm which base, accessories and instructions belong in the gift.
- Use an insert that supports the object and lets the recipient lift it out easily.

## Super Slide: choose a personal puzzle, with an easy first attempt

[GiiKER Super Slide](/#gift-giiker-slide) is a sliding-block puzzle: the player follows a layout and moves the pieces to bring the red block to its target. That makes the decision about enjoying a challenge, rather than simply displaying a designed object.

It is a useful direction to compare for a puzzle-minded colleague or a personal Eid gift. During sample review, ask someone unfamiliar with the product to set up and begin a puzzle using the supplied instructions. Confirm how pieces are kept together between uses and whether the controls suit the intended setting.

- Check the exact version, instruction language and manufacturer age guidance.
- Confirm the power arrangement and what is supplied with the unit.
- Try setup, moving the pieces and putting the game away, not only its appearance.

## Smart Four: plan for a shared table and a complete set

[GiiKER Smart Four](/#gift-giiker-four) uses a three-dimensional four-in-a-row format, with head-to-head play and an AI opponent. Shared play is a useful reason to shortlist it for a household gift or a company family activity.

For an Eid campaign, distinguish a gift for the employee from a gift intended for the household. For an event, distinguish a demonstration unit used at a table from a boxed gift taken away. They may use the same model but need different quantities, instructions and packing arrangements.

- Check the room needed for the board, players and stored pieces.
- Count the contents and confirm the supplied power arrangement on the approved sample.
- Review manufacturer age guidance for the intended users; a family theme alone does not establish suitability.

## Match the UAE occasion without changing the product into a slogan

For employee recognition, connect the gift to the person’s interest and a specific message of appreciation. For an innovation launch, a kinetic desk object can support the design theme, while a game may suit a planned participation activity. Choose the activity first so the item has a purpose beyond carrying a logo.

For Eid Al Fitr or Eid Al Adha, write the greeting for that occasion and decide whether the gift is personal or shared. For Flag Day or Eid Al Etihad, a restrained sleeve and Arabic–English card can connect the presentation to the campaign. Use our [UAE gifting calendar](/guides/uae-corporate-gifting-calendar/) for planning and our [Ramadan and Eid guide](/guides/ramadan-eid-corporate-gifts/) for recipient distinctions.

- Agree the Arabic and English recipient name before personalised printing.
- Preserve the branded product’s instructions and required labels.
- Confirm product decoration separately; an outer box, sleeve and card can carry the company identity.

## Approve a usable gift, then plan the handover

Review one assembled sample with the actual product, accessories, instructions, greeting and fitted insert. Record the approved model and finish, then assign an owner to product approval and another to recipient names if different teams handle them. A photograph of an empty box cannot settle how the complete gift fits.

For several branches or an event, separate demonstration stock from recipient gifts in the quantity plan. Agree carton labels, the receiving contact and the required handover date. We confirm availability and the project schedule for your selected products and presentation.

Send the occasion, recipient groups, preferred option, quantities and handover locations through our [gift project brief](/#gift-brief). For packaging and original development options, see [what MUSE can create](/guides/corporate-gift-customisation/).

- Product: exact version, finish, complete contents and usable instructions.
- Experience: start, use, reset or stop, and pack away on the sample.
- Presentation: name proof, greeting, secure fit and easy removal.
- Handover: item counts by location, agreed dates and a named recipient or receiving owner.
