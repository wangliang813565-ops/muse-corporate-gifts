## Two gifts with different everyday roles

Start with where the gift will be used. A field-sales colleague may need to top up a laptop between appointments; a colleague at a fixed workstation may value one charging station for a laptop, phone and tablet. Ask which situation matters, then record the actual device models.

The [Anker Prime power bank](/#gift-anker-powerbank), model A1340, stores energy in a 27,650 mAh battery and has two USB-C ports and one USB-A port. The [Prime desktop charger](/#gift-anker-desktop), model A2345, uses a mains connection and provides four USB-C and two USB-A ports. It is a desk power source, not a battery to use away from an outlet.

## What does 250W mean for the recipient?

The 250W figure is the maximum combined output, not 250W available from every port. On the desktop model, USB-C1 is rated up to 140W; the other USB-C ports are rated up to 100W each, subject to the shared power allocation. The power bank also has a maximum single USB-C output of 140W.

Specify a useful task rather than selecting by the largest number: for example, charging the intended work laptop while the phone is connected. Confirm the laptop accepts USB-C charging and check its required power, the chosen port and the cable rating. Connecting another device can change the power available to the first one; approve the combination that will actually be used.

## Approve the accessories with the product

For the portable option, confirm how the recipient will recharge the power bank. Review the proposed charging adapter and cable as part of the complete gift, and handle the sample to judge its carrying size and weight. For air travel, check the operating airline’s current battery rules for the exact item and itinerary before selecting it as a travel gift.

For the desktop option, confirm the regional mains cord, socket fit and cable reach to the intended devices. Plan where the unit will sit so the recipient can read the display and reach its ports. Keep the sample’s labels and operating instructions accessible.

- Record exact model, regional package, colour and included accessories.
- Connect the intended laptop and phone together using the proposed cables.
- Check display, controls and reconnection in the intended setup.
- Confirm the support route and retain the approved package specification for packing.

## Match the presentation to Eid, recognition or an office opening

For Eid employee appreciation, offer a portable or desk-based choice and use one coordinated Arabic–English greeting. For year-end client thanks, choose around the recipient’s working routine and personalise the card with the approved name. An office opening gives the desktop model a clear setting: a useful addition to a new workstation.

Confirm recipient choices first, followed by artwork, the assembled sample, packing and handover. Each project’s schedule depends on its approved specification.
