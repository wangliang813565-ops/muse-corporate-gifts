## Give Flag Day its own team brief

UAE Flag Day is observed annually on 3 November. For your company, begin with the actual gathering or staff handover date and the people taking part. Plan the gifting alongside that event; use the UAE gifting calendar to keep this brief separate from your December Eid Al Etihad campaign.

A team may include office colleagues, engineers, travelling staff and people working different shifts. A coordinated presentation can express a shared occasion while the contents reflect those different everyday lives. Decide whether colleagues choose from a shortlist or whether procurement assigns items by a clear, relevant preference.

- Confirm the company's event and handover dates, locations and approximate quantities.
- Name one person to approve the gift choices and one to coordinate each receiving location.

## Compare products by the person who will use them

A personalised leather folio is a useful direction for someone who carries meeting notes and documents. Confirm its format and what the recipient actually carries. The EKETOP kinetic metal top is a more playful desk direction for a colleague interested in design and mechanical objects; check that a desk display suits their working space.

For colleagues who enjoy practical tools, compare the HOTO electric screwdriver kit. Its appeal depends on that interest, rather than simply an engineering job title. A Laifen Wave toothbrush offers a personal-care direction for colleagues who welcome that type of gift. An individual preference check is especially useful before choosing personal-care items for a team.

- Meetings and documents: explore the leather folio.
- Design and desk play: explore the EKETOP metal top.
- Practical making: explore the HOTO kit.
- Personal care: offer Laifen as a preference-led option.

## Create one presentation system

Bring the shortlist together with a consistent outer-box style, coordinated materials and one card design. Box sizes and inserts should follow the actual products: a folio and a metal top need different support. Review how each item is held, removed and carried before approving the presentation family.

For selected consumer-brand products, put your campaign identity on the presentation box, sleeve or card. Discuss product decoration separately where it is permitted. Our corporate gift customisation guide explains the difference between branded presentation, personalisation and original manufacture.

- Approve one visual direction across every gift variant.
- Check a complete packed sample for each different product or insert.

## Proof names and greetings in the final layout

Choose an Arabic greeting or a bilingual Arabic–English message to suit your team. Ask the nominated company reviewer to confirm the wording, name spelling and layout together. Keep each recipient's approved spelling paired with their chosen gift; a correct name on the wrong box still creates a handover problem.

Use one reviewed list with a recipient reference, gift choice, approved name text, language and delivery location. Inspect long names and mixed Arabic–English text at the final print or engraving size. Keep an unnamed presentation option for recipients who are confirmed after the named artwork has been approved.

- Confirm the final name list and who approves changes.
- Review card, sleeve and any permitted personalisation as one matched set.

## Plan the last handover as carefully as the first sample

Count gifts by variant and receiving location, then agree how cartons will be labelled so each branch can distribute the correct packs. Named gifts need a matching method that the receiving colleague can use without opening every presentation box. Account for different shifts, travelling staff and gifts collected after the main event.

Confirm the supply, sample, production and transport plan against your actual company date. The occasion's date alone does not establish that a particular quantity or personalisation route can be delivered. Share your locations and handover requirements while the shortlist is being reviewed, rather than after packaging is settled.

- Reconcile the approved quantities against the branch packing list.
- Confirm who receives each shipment and how the named gifts reach their recipients.
