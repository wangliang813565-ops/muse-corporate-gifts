## Start with the recipient, then choose the occasion

Personal care works best as an individual gift with a known preference. For Eid Al Fitr employee appreciation, invite recipients to select an option privately before quantities are finalised. For a service anniversary or year-end thank-you, choose the device the individual will use. A valued client can receive the same thoughtful treatment when their preference is known.

For a Ramadan host visit, first establish whether you are thanking the individual or bringing something for the household. Our [Ramadan and Eid guide](/guides/ramadan-eid-corporate-gifts/) helps separate those briefs. Keep a [desk or interactive gift](/blog/eketop-giiker-desk-gift-comparison/) available for people who prefer something outside personal care; a simple choice is enough, without asking for health information.

## Wave: make the charging cable and replacement heads part of the choice

The [Wave electric toothbrush](/#gift-laifen-wave) is the option for a recipient interested in that daily routine. Laifen’s US product page specifies a magnetic-to-USB-C charging cable, model-specific brush heads and no power adapter in the box. Confirm the contents of the exact regional version in the proposal.

Agree the supplied heads and any additional heads, colour, compatible power adapter if requested, and where the recipient can obtain replacements. Keep the charger accessible in the fitted packaging. A Wave, Wave Pro or another edition should have its own named contents list rather than sharing an assumed specification.

## P3 Pro: choose for an electric-shaver user

The [P3 Pro electric shaver](/#gift-laifen-p3) offers a different personal-care route. Laifen describes a three-blade shaver with a CNC-machined aluminium body. Its US listing includes a USB-C cable and protective cap; a power adapter is excluded and the travel pod is optional. Confirm the selected bundle before showing a complete travel set.

For a frequent traveller, decide whether the brief needs the protective cap alone or a separate carrying option. For personal recognition, concentrate on the finish and presentation the recipient likes. Review how the selected unit is charged, cleaned and stored, and include the relevant instructions with the gift.

## Give two product choices one coherent company presentation

Use one campaign identity across both options: a company sleeve, a fitted presentation and an Arabic–English card. The insert should fit the chosen retail box and accessories. Keep the original product packaging and recipient units sealed, with the company identity carried by the outer presentation.

MUSE coordinates the product selection and packaging design around your approved brief. Any marking on the device needs separate confirmation of placement and method. Our [customisation guide](/guides/corporate-gift-customisation/) explains the options for branded products and original development. For named gifts, match the selected model, recipient name and greeting before packing.

## Approve one complete kit for each selected model

Use a separate demonstration unit to review handling and charging. Recipient units should be unused and sealed. Record the accepted kit and packaging so the final order refers to a complete gift, not just a product photograph.

- **Product:** exact model, regional version, finish and recipient option.
- **Contents:** supplied accessories, any added consumables or carrying case, charging arrangement and instructions.
- **Presentation:** retail-box fit, accessory storage, company sleeve, card language and name matching.
- **Support:** confirmed replacement-consumable route, service contact and warranty terms for the supplied region; do not assume another market’s retail terms apply.
- **Approval:** sample reference, accepted contents list and final artwork version for each option.

The company distribution date belongs in the brief even when it differs from the holiday itself.
