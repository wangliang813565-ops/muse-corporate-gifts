## Your gift project, from Dubai to the making details {#gift-team}

Our corporate gift team works with UAE buyers on what to give, how it should carry the company identity and what must be approved before production. We coordinate sourcing and custom development with suppliers in China, bringing the chosen product, packaging and delivery requirements into one project brief.

We offer three routes: a recognised product with branded presentation; personalisation on a suitable existing item; or an original object developed around your identity. The [customisation guide](/guides/corporate-gift-customisation/) explains the drawings, artwork and samples needed for each route.

## Products with a purpose

For everyday use, compare technology such as Laifen personal care, Anker charging equipment and Shokz audio. For recipients who enjoy design and play, explore EKETOP kinetic desk pieces or GiiKER puzzles. For hosting and recognition, consider coffee pieces, leather folios or an original MUSE architectural oud burner.

Our [29-gift collection](/#gift-selection) connects these choices to recipients and occasions. Ramadan hospitality, Eid appreciation and Eid Al Etihad recognition call for different gifts and greetings. Start with the recipient's interests and the actual handover date; use our [UAE occasion guide](/guides/uae-corporate-gifting-calendar/) to plan the sequence.

## What you approve before production {#project-approval}

- **Product:** the exact model or drawing, quantity by variant, materials or finish, included accessories and regional support terms.
- **Branding:** your logo placement, Arabic–English names and greetings, decoration method and artwork proof.
- **Presentation:** box dimensions, fitted inserts, cards and the complete assembled sample.
- **Delivery:** the agreed production milestones, packing quantities, delivery destination and required handover date.

Your project quotation and order confirmation set out the agreed scope and commercial terms. Branding on established products begins with the packaging and cards; any change to the product itself is checked for that model. For original pieces, the physical sample is the reference for approving form and finish.
