## Your gift brief and contact details

Choosing products and preparing a project brief happens in your browser. Your shortlist is stored for the browser session. The form lets you review the brief before opening WhatsApp; it does not submit it to a website form database.

When you choose to open WhatsApp with the prepared brief, that text passes to WhatsApp. You then choose whether to send the message to MUSE. When you contact us by WhatsApp or email, we receive the contact and project information you send and use it to discuss your request, prepare a proposal or support your order.

For the initial enquiry, send the project requirements rather than recipient lists, identity documents or payment details. We discuss any information needed for personalisation or delivery as part of your project.

## Optional visit analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you choose **Allow analytics**. You can decline and continue using the gift catalogue, shortlist and contact links.

If you accept, Google receives page visits, the referring website, browser and device information, approximate location and cookie identifiers. We use these to understand which pages and visit sources help buyers find gifts. Google may process the data outside your country. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We also measure clicks on our WhatsApp consultation buttons, including the page and button position. A button click does not tell us whether a message was sent. We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, gift-brief text or WhatsApp message content to Analytics. Advertising features and automatic form tracking are disabled. Page query strings and fragments are omitted from analytics, and the referring website is reduced to its origin.

## Browser storage and changing your choice

The site remembers your analytics choice in this browser for up to **180 days**. Analytics cookies expire after 180 days without automatic renewal; your browser may remove them sooner.

Use **Cookie settings** in the footer to change your choice. Declining stops future collection by this site's Analytics integration and clears its analytics cookies in the current browser. It does not erase data already received by Google. Your gift shortlist uses separate session storage to keep selections while you browse.

## Hosting and external services

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting requests can include your IP address, browser information and the requested resource, independently of optional Analytics. See the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Following a WhatsApp, Instagram, manufacturer or other external link takes you to that service, which handles information under its own privacy practices. WhatsApp's information is available in its [privacy policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy).

Describe the relevant project or correspondence so we can identify the request; do not email identity or payment documents with the initial enquiry.
