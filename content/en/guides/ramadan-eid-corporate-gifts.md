## Define three briefs, even when one team manages them

A Ramadan client visit, an employee's Eid Al Fitr gift and an Eid Al Adha partner greeting have different purposes. A hospitality piece may be useful to a host, but it need not be the right choice for every colleague. Religious occasions do not make personal preferences identical.

Set the recipient group, intended use and actual handover for each brief. Decide whether the gift belongs to an individual, a household or a shared business setting. That distinction shapes the product, greeting, packaging and quantity.

- Ramadan: welcoming, hosting and an agreed visit or gathering.
- Eid Al Fitr: appreciation for an individual or something a family can enjoy together.
- Eid Al Adha: a separate partner or client gesture with its own greeting and handover.

## Ramadan hosts: compare an oud burner with a qahwa service

Our [architectural oud burners](/#gift-oud-majlis) offer three design directions: tower, curved wall and courtyard. Choose the silhouette that fits the host's space and the project's visual language. Then compare metal finish, leather colour and presentation. Agree dimensions, heat-handling components and use instructions through a physical sample.

The [contemporary qahwa service](/#gift-ceramic-qahwa) takes a different role: a ceramic-and-brass direction for serving Arabic coffee. Confirm the pieces and number of cups around the host's actual use. [Glass hospitality pieces](/#gift-glass-hospitality) are another serving direction, while a [personal leather folio](/#gift-leather-folio) may better suit a business host whose gift should stay useful at work.

Plan to hand over a hospitality gift before the gathering where it is intended to be used. An impressive package that arrives after the visit misses that purpose.

- Oud burner: architectural form, handling, finish and approved sample.
- Qahwa or glass: serving pieces, care instructions and protective packing.
- Folio: document format, internal layout and the recipient's approved name.

## Eid Al Fitr: distinguish employee appreciation from family play

For an individual, compare [Shokz OpenFit 2](/#gift-shokz-openfit) or [Huawei FreeClip 2](/#gift-huawei-freeclip) by wearing preference and phone compatibility. [Laifen Wave](/#gift-laifen-wave) can fit a personal-care gift when that is welcome. A [leather folio](/#gift-leather-folio) offers a work-related alternative.

For family time, [GiiKER Smart Four](/#gift-giiker-four) is a shared strategy-game direction; [GiiKER Super Slide](/#gift-giiker-slide) offers an individual puzzle challenge. [ROKR Marble Spaceport](/#gift-rokr-spaceport) suits a recipient who wants an assembly project. Check the manufacturer's age guidance and the actual instructions rather than treating every game as suitable for every household.

An [EKETOP kinetic top](/#gift-eketop) can bring a playful moment to a design-minded adult's desk. Match the gift to the person, then use a coordinated Eid Al Fitr card and box to connect the team campaign.

- Record whether each gift is for the employee or for family use.
- Offer different useful products within the same presentation when preferences vary.
- Confirm names and distribution quantities before colleagues leave for the holiday.

## Eid Al Adha partners: choose around a known interest

For a partner who enjoys hosting, revisit the [architectural oud burner](/#gift-oud-majlis). For a traveller, consider the [leather weekender](/#gift-leather-weekender) after checking dimensions, handles, strap and lining. For someone who enjoys preparing coffee, compare [Wacaco Picopresso](/#gift-wacaco-pico) with the [TIMEMORE Chestnut S3 hand grinder](/#gift-timemore-s3).

Coffee equipment and a qahwa service answer different needs: preparation for an enthusiast versus serving guests. A [Huawei WATCH GT 6 · 46 mm](/#gift-huawei-watch) or another quality technology choice can suit an individual partner when fit and device compatibility are known. Use our [technology gift guide](/guides/premium-technology-gifts/) to make that check specific.

Use an Eid Al Adha message and confirm whether the recipient will be available for the intended handover. Travel or leave arrangements can matter more than a general office delivery date.

- Hosting: consider the recipient's space and use of the object.
- Travel, coffee or technology: choose the activity the recipient actually enjoys.
- Confirm the named recipient, location and handover contact.

## Make the Arabic greeting, name and packaging work together

Keep the occasion clear in the wording. A Ramadan greeting might use «رمضان كريم»; an Eid Al Fitr or Eid Al Adha card should name the relevant Eid when the campaign needs that distinction. Let the message of appreciation sound natural in Arabic rather than translating an English slogan word for word.

Prepare one approved recipient list with the Arabic and English names, titles where needed, product choice and destination. Review spelling and right-to-left layout in the artwork, then inspect the name and greeting on the final material. Separate a company-wide card from any personalised detail so late name corrections do not disturb the whole design.

Choose a fitted box or a [woven presentation case](/#gift-woven-hospitality) around the actual contents. Check the opening sequence, secure inserts, accessories and instructions together. Use a restrained company identity that leaves the greeting and gift easy to enjoy.

- Name one owner for recipient data and one final artwork approver.
- Approve Arabic and English text, name placement, material and colour together.
- Match each personalised pack to its recipient before cartons are closed.

## Choose the development route before approving the schedule

For a branded technology item, start with the exact model and regional package, then agree the outer box, sleeve and greeting. Confirm any decoration on the product separately. For an original oud burner or crafted piece, the form, materials and finish require their own development and sample decisions.

Our China sourcing and development work connects consumer technology, precision metalwork, leather, ceramic and glass, and coordinated packaging. A mixed gift set needs one assembled sample to confirm that those elements fit and travel together. It also needs clear decisions on what is included.

Use our [customisation guide](/guides/corporate-gift-customisation/) to compare these routes. If the handover is close, compare an established product with a simpler presentation before committing to a new manufactured form.

- Approve product configuration and included accessories.
- Approve artwork, names, box structure and inserts.
- Approve the complete sample, inspection points and packing plan before production.

## Work backwards from the real handover

Set a working handover date around your first Ramadan gathering, employees' final working day before Eid Al Fitr, or a partner's availability for Eid Al Adha. Follow UAE official announcements for Ramadan and Eid dates, and review your plan when those dates are confirmed. A planning date is not a guaranteed delivery commitment.

Build the project schedule with us from that handover backwards. Allow for sample revisions, recipient proofing, the selected production route, factory holiday schedules in China and transport. The approved proposal should give each stage an owner and a date.

Use the [UAE gifting calendar](/guides/uae-corporate-gifting-calendar/) for the broader annual plan and the [seven-category selection guide](/guides/corporate-gift-selection/) for alternatives. Share the occasion, recipient groups, quantities, preferred products, required handover and destinations through the [gift project brief](/#gift-brief).

- Handover: named person, location, date and recipient availability.
- Receiving and distribution: time for carton checks, name matching and branch or team allocation.
- Transport and packing: agreed route, protective packing and confirmed dispatch plan.
- Production: approved configuration, sample and inspection points.
- Approvals and selection: final names, bilingual artwork, materials and product choice, with room to correct a sample.
